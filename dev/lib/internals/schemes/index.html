Scheme representations and verification

An explicit rule stores its coefficient tables. The Shpilka representation stores a program and can return deferred coefficient queries. Verification reports record which identity was checked and which exponent bound was obtained.

Scheme types and coefficient access

NineFourths.ExplicitScheme — Type
ExplicitScheme(u, U, V, W)

Store a block side u and three coefficient tables with u² rows.

The type parameters retain the individual table types. Use BilinearScheme to infer u and validate the table shapes and axes.

NineFourths.ShpilkaProgram — Type
ShpilkaProgram(steps, log_length, power, copies, overhead, operations)

Store the finite construction and its integer bounds from §6 of Shpilka's exposition.

steps bounds the number of derivation steps, and log_length bounds the base-two logarithm of their maximum length. power gives the witness tensor power, copies counts the seed copies, and overhead is the coefficient of the extra seed-copy term. The three operations materialize a derivation, compile a witness, and decompose the polynomial seed in that order.

Verification reports and checks

NineFourths.RankExponent — Type
RankExponent(base, rank)

Represent the exact exponent bound log_base(rank) from a checked coefficient table.

base is the integer block side of at least two, and rank is the product count.

source
NineFourths.VerificationReport — Type
VerificationReport

Report the evidence for a bilinear rule's identity and exponent bound.

The symbol identity is :exact_coefficients after a coefficient-by-coefficient check, :symbolic_restriction after a construction certificate check, :failed for a failed check, or :unchecked if enumeration exceeds its budget. exponent holds a RankExponent for a verified table with block side at least two, a Rational{BigInt} bound for a symbolic certificate, or nothing when a bound is unavailable. target holds the requested Shpilka exponent as a Rational{BigInt} when present. valid is true when the identity and any requested exponent bound are verified. diagnostics contains strings explaining a failed or skipped check.
