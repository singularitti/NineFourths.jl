Witness compiler

The compiler assembles restrictions, extracts coefficients by interpolation, and handles the auxiliary matrix tensor. Coefficients can be evaluated from this representation without storing dense tables for the full witness.

NineFourths.CompiledWitness — Type
CompiledWitness(side, copies, restriction, recipe)

Represent an exact restriction from a seed power to matrix multiplication.

side is the matrix dimension and copies counts the seed factors in the source. The recipe is a WitnessRecipe when the witness can be rebuilt from a derivation, or nothing for a supplied restriction.

NineFourths._WitnessEvent — Type
_WitnessEvent(index, copies, counts, multiplicity, child_side, side)

Store the copy counts and matrix sides for one step in the reversed derivation.

index identifies the derivation node and copies counts the seed factors processed there. counts holds the three sector counts for a tripling step or the two midpoint counts followed by zero. multiplicity counts the sector words with those counts. For other steps, all copies use the first count and the multiplicity is one. child_side and side give the matrix side before and after accounting for this step's gain and auxiliary factors.

NineFourths._compile_round — Function
_compile_round(nodes, plan, auxiliary, N, S, admissible_order=nothing)

Build the restriction for one reuse of the powered auxiliary tensor.

plan comes from _witness_events. The source has N^2 seed copies, 18S*N budget copies, and the factors in auxiliary. The target has a matrix multiplication factor of side plan.side^N followed by the returned auxiliary factors in their original order. Pass admissible_order to select Fourier orders for the interpolated phases.

NineFourths._compile_sector! — Function
_compile_sector!(layout, nodes, event, N, remaining, a, b, admissible_order=nothing)

Apply both interpolated phases of a midpoint or tripling event to layout.

Use N replicas of the event identified by nodes and event. Draw unit factors from the seed at (a, b), using the optional predicate admissible_order to choose Fourier orders in each phase. Assign the resulting seed copies to the predecessors, record the matrix gain, and return the updated BigInt seed allowance.

NineFourths._interpolated_phase — Method
_interpolated_phase(transition, phase, N; admissible_order=nothing)

Extract the constant term of a sector phase after taking its Nth tensor power.

phase selects stage 1 or 2 of transition. All copies of the phase use the same evaluation point. If the powered stage has degree bound D, the default evaluates at the integers from 1 to D + 1 with signed binomial weights.

Supply admissible_order(n)::Bool to select invertible Fourier orders. The interpolated phase then averages over all roots of unity of the selected order, which exceeds D. Return the sample count points and the resulting TensorRestriction.

NineFourths._witness_copies — Method
_witness_copies(S, N)::BigInt

Count the seed copies reserved for a derivation with step bound S and power N.

Preparation costs 16S*N^2 copies. Each of the N reuse rounds takes N^2 copies plus a budget of 18S*N. Per step, the budget allows 6N copies for the unit factor and 6N for each interpolation phase. The interpolation allowance follows from 6N*9^N + 2 ≤ 64^N.

NineFourths._witness_events — Method
_witness_events(nodes, N) -> (; events, baseline, side)

Allocate N copies among the dependencies of the final node in Lemma 6.7.

Supply a one-based indexed sequence of DerivationNodes in dependency order and a positive integer N. The returned events list contains _WitnessEvents in reverse derivation order. The baseline list pairs node indices with positive BigInt copy counts. side is the resulting BigInt matrix side before the final power.

NineFourths.compile_witness — Method
compile_witness(nodes, N; step_bound=nothing, admissible_order=nothing)

Lower a derivation to the exact restriction in Lemma 6.2. Interpolation is performed separately on the two phases of each sector transition after taking the Nth tensor power. The powered auxiliary tensor is prepared once, returned by each of N applications, and then restricted to a scalar.

Supply a finite iterable of DerivationNode entries in dependency order and a positive integer power N. The derivation is collected into a Vector{DerivationNode}. An integer step_bound must be at least the number of non-baseline entries. The power, step bound, and seed-copy counts are BigInts in the compiler.

When provided, admissible_order(order) takes a BigInt Fourier order and returns a Bool indicating whether that order is invertible in the coefficient field. This also interpolates every phase at roots of unity.

Return a CompiledWitness with a BigInt matrix side and seed-copy count, the TensorRestriction, and the WitnessRecipe used to build it.

source
NineFourths.witness_side — Method
witness_side(nodes, N)

Calculate the matrix side in Lemma 6.2 without instantiating restriction maps. This evaluates the binomial and multinomial multiplicities and the final power. Supply a one-based indexed sequence of derivation entries in dependency order and a positive integer power N. The entries are DerivationNodes and the result is a BigInt matrix side after raising the planned side to N^2.
